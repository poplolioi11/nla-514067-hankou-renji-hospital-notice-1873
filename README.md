# 《中西聞見錄》〈漢口花樓仁濟醫院敬啟〉（1873 年，澳洲國家圖書館 514067） / Hankou Hua lou Ren ji yi yuan jing qi [Notice from Renji Hospital at Hualou, Hankou]: Transcription Dataset

〈漢口花樓仁濟醫院敬啟〉刊於《中西聞見錄》第十四號（1873 年 9 月）。院方說明依病情輕重安排患者住院或帶藥回家、免費施醫送藥，以及原址因水患、周圍衛生和病房容量受限而籌募經費遷院。編刊者的〈漢口近事〉引言與院方告示各自標明，兩者均錄至篇末。底本由澳洲國家圖書館收藏，影像由海德堡大學 Early Chinese Periodicals Online 提供。

Published in The Peking Magazine (Zhong xi wen jian lu) no. 14 in September 1873, this notice from Renji Hospital at Hualou in Hankou describes free care, decisions about admitting patients or sending them home with medicine, and the effect of flooding, sanitation and limited bed space on care. The complete notice and the journal's separate introductory item are transcribed from the National Library of Australia copy, accessed through Heidelberg University's Early Chinese Periodicals Online.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。各半由右至左，篇題與欄位原樣保存。可見原刻點轉成頓點。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Read each half from right to left, starting on the right. The print’s comparable punctuation marks are represented as ideographic commas. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending.
